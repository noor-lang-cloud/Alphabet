1. The English Alphabet

↑ Contents

26 letters. For each letter: listen to its name, its sound, then practise the example words and the sentence.

2. Vowel Sounds: a e i o u

↑ Contents

Each vowel has a short sound and a long sound (the long sound says its own name). A silent e at the end of a word (cake, bike, home) often makes the vowel long: the “magic e”.

3. Vowel Teams & R-Sounds

↑ Contents

When two vowels walk together, they make one new sound.

4. Special Consonant Sounds

↑ Contents

Two or more letters can make one sound. The orange letters show the sound.

5. Word Endings: tion, sion, ture, su

↑ Contents

These endings are very common. Learn them as one chunk.

6. “dh” and “kh” in Foreign Words

↑ Contents

“dh” and “kh” are not normal English spellings. You will only see them in names and words borrowed from other languages. The English sounds you may be looking for are th (this) and k/h.

7. Reading Practice

↑ Contents

Listen first (🔊 normal, 🐢 slow), then press 🎤 and read the whole sentence.

8. Useful Words كلمات مفيدة

↑ Contents

Common words for daily life, work and conversation. Each word has its Arabic meaning, an example sentence and its Arabic translation. Listen 🔊 and say it 🎤.

English Hub · Phonics Book

  • 🔊 The sounds come from the voice installed on your phone or computer, so the book works without internet when an English voice is installed.
  • 🎤 Chrome and Edge use an online service to check speech, so scoring needs internet. Without it, the book records your voice, plays it back, then plays the model so you can compare.
  • The first time you press 🎤, allow the microphone. Not working? Press “Test microphone” at the top of the page. The microphone needs a secure page: open the book from an https link (Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages) or in Chrome, not from a WhatsApp / Drive / file-manager preview.
  • 🌐 No internet? Press “Prepare offline mode” once while you are online. It saves the English speech model on your device, and after that the 🎤 buttons can check your words without internet.
  • Speech engines: Auto uses the browser’s Google speech recognition and switches to Whisper AI (open-source, runs inside the browser) if it fails.
  • ✅ Say the word: if it is right you see “صحيح · Correct”, if not you see “غلط · Wrong”. The line like /ˈæpəl/ under each word is the American English pronunciation (IPA).
